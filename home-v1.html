Engineering services

When the part you need isn't on a shelf

We are a supplier with a workshop behind it. The same team that answers the phone about a 4–20 mA loop also designs the board, writes the PLC code and builds the panel.

01

Custom hardware design

Circuit and PCB design through to a housed, labelled, DIN-mountable product — the way our own KTA range was built.

02

PLC programming

Delta, Teco, Akytec and HNC platforms — new machine logic, retrofits, and rescuing undocumented code someone else left behind.

03

HMI & SCADA screens

Operator interfaces that a shift worker can read at a glance, wired through Modbus, BACnet or MQTT to whatever is already installed.

04

Embedded & data acquisition

Logging, remote monitoring and test rigs — like the 25-tonne hydraulic press we instrumented with a LabJack U6 for an Australian compliance lab.

About Ocean Controls

A supply shop run by engineers, in Carrum Downs

You can walk into our store on Frankston Gardens Drive, Monday to Friday, and talk to someone who knows the difference between a PT100 and a thermocouple. We hold stock rather than drop-ship, we publish the datasheet next to the price, and we ship worldwide through distributors in nine countries.

Ninety-three of the products we sell carry our own name — designed here, built here, and still supported by the person who drew the schematic.
