02 — Reselling our range

If you resell, talk to us first

System integrators, panel builders and equipment makers buy from us on trade terms rather than at retail. Volume matters less than whether the parts suit what you build, so the conversation starts with the application.

  • Contract pricing across the catalogue once approved
  • Consolidated shipping, several manufacturers on one consignment
  • Datasheets and configuration files for everything you resell
  • An engineer on the phone, not a call centre
