A supply shop with a working engineering shop behind it.

Most suppliers sell you a part and wish you luck. We hold 2,279 products on our own shelves in Carrum Downs — and when the catalogue does not have the answer, the same engineers design the board, write the PLC code and commission the machine.

01 — Who we are

We stock what we sell, and we support what we build

Ocean Controls supplies industrial automation and control equipment across Australia from a warehouse and shopfront at 44 Frankston Gardens Drive, Carrum Downs. You can order online, phone us, email a purchase order, or walk in and talk to someone across the counter.

We try to keep at least one to three of every in-stock item on the shelf, so the quantity shown on a product page is a real figure from a real building — not a supplier feed. Anything not on the shelf goes straight onto our purchasing list, and some of it comes from overseas, so we tell you the lead time in writing rather than leaving you guessing.

Alongside the catalogue we run an engineering practice: engineering design, software, support and training for the industrial and technical sectors. Ninety-three of the products we sell came out of that practice and carry our own name.
